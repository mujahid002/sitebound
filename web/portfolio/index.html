About Me

Backend and smart-contract engineer, 3+ years in blockchain. Since Mar 2025 I’ve been a core engineer at a VC-backed stablecoin payments network on Base, live in 15+ markets, that lets people pay and get paid in USDC every day. I own its pricing (Chainlink CRE, prices on-chain every minute), the keeper service, the payment-proof service, the alerting pipeline, the support AI and much of the infrastructure they run on, and I’m one of the core Solidity engineers on its contracts: matching, disputes, limits, pricing and insurance.

I sit closest to production: when a payment stalls or an event goes missing, I trace it on-chain to the root cause and ship the fix.

Before that I wrote Solidity for the Knowledge Protocol at GetBoarded and an ERC-20 checkout gateway at Hooman Digital, and won prizes at ETHGlobal, PermaHacks and Base hackathons.
