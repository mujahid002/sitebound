Sitebound

Set up once, then pay with Touch ID

Three steps, about a minute. After this, each payment on any website is one Touch ID.

  1. Connect walletYour USDT stays in your wallet until you pay.
  2. Set limitsCaps on what Sitebound can ever take.
  3. Confirm with Touch IDTouch ID creates your passkey, then your wallet signs twice.

Any browser wallet. Whitechain Testnet (1874) is added if needed.

Wallet

Limits

Amounts are in USDT. Defaults, edit any of them:

  • 50 per payment: no single payment can be larger.
  • 200 total: all payments together stop here, until you set up again.
  • 30 days: after that the passkey stops working. You can revoke it sooner.

Sitebound can never move more than these limits, and only to the website you are paying on.

  1. Create your passkey (Touch ID)
  2. Allow USDT up to your total (wallet signature)
  3. Lock in your limits (wallet signature)
  4. Save it on Whitechain
