Three ways crypto checkout goes wrong. One fix each.

  1. 1

    ProblemHacked checkout code swaps the address.

    Fix

    Every payment is bound to the site the buyer is on.

    The buyer's browser signs which site they are on, and the contract pays only the wallet registered for that site. Hacked checkout code can change what the page says, but not who gets paid.

  2. 2

    ProblemAddresses make checkout painful.

    Fix

    Addressless.

    The shop never puts an address in its code, and the buyer never sees one. Nothing to copy, paste or compare.

  3. 3

    ProblemCrypto has no 3-D Secure.

    Fix

    A confirmation frame that shows the site and the amount.

    The frame shows the site as reported by the browser, and the amount. The buyer confirms with Touch ID, and spending caps apply.

For shops

  1. Type your domain

    The site you own, like shop.com.

  2. Add one DNS TXT record

    Proves you control the domain, once.

  3. Paste a ~20-line snippet

    Your checkout opens the Sitebound frame. No address in your code.

For buyers

  1. First time

    Connect your wallet and create a passkey.

  2. After that: Touch ID

    Check the site and the amount, then confirm.
