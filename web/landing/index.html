How it works

    Register

    Add your domain and one DNS record.

  2. 02

    Confirm

    Like 3-D Secure, Touch ID signs the site and amount.

  3. 03

    Get paid

    No "pay to" field: USDT goes only to your site's wallet. Buyers pay a 0.01 USDT fee, no gas.

Honest limits

  • One trusted checker.

    Our relayer alone checks DNS and signs registrations.

  • Site owners hold power.

    They can freeze payments or cut a partner's share instantly.

  • Not phishing protection.

    A fake site or overlay can fool buyers, not change who a real site pays.
