Why we are building it

  1. Copying an address.

    One wrong paste is final. Source

  2. Injected code.

    A bad Ledger library update cost users ~$600k (2023). Source

  3. No 3-D Secure.

    Nothing trusted shows the real site and amount. Our view

  4. Hard for web2.

    Starting needs a crypto team. Our view

  5. Gas.

    Paying USDT also costs the chain's coin. Source

How it works

  1. Register

    Add your domain and one DNS record.

  2. Confirm

    Like 3-D Secure, Touch ID signs the site and amount. The browser names the site, not your page.

  3. Get paid

    No "pay to" field: USDT goes only to your site's wallet. Buyers pay a 0.01 USDT fee, no gas.

A hacked page cannot redirect the money.

Honest limits

  • One trusted checker.

    Our relayer alone checks DNS and signs registrations.

  • Site owners hold power.

    They can freeze payments or cut a partner's share instantly.

  • Not phishing protection.

    A fake site or overlay can fool buyers, not change who a real site pays.
