Three ways crypto checkout fails

  1. 01

    ProblemHacked checkout code swaps the address.

    Fix

    Every payment is bound to the site the buyer is on.

    The buyer's browser signs which site they are on, and the contract pays only the wallet registered for that site. Hacked checkout code can change what the page says, but not who gets paid.

  2. 02

    ProblemAddresses make checkout painful.

    Fix

    Addressless.

    Your site never puts an address in its code, and the buyer never sees one. Nothing to copy, paste or compare.

  3. 03

    ProblemCrypto has no 3-D Secure.

    Fix

    A confirmation frame that shows the site and the amount.

    The frame shows the site as reported by the browser, and the amount. The buyer confirms with Touch ID, and spending caps apply.

How a payment is routed

  1. Browser

    Signs the site it is on

    The origin the buyer is on, never an address from the page.

  2. Contract

    Looks up that site

    Only the wallet registered for the site counts.

  3. Wallet

    Gets paid

    USDT goes straight from the buyer. The contract holds nothing.

For website owners

  1. Type your domain

    The site you own, like example.com.

  2. Add one DNS TXT record

    Proves you control the domain, once.

  3. Paste a ~20-line snippet

    Your checkout opens the Sitebound frame. No address in your code.

For buyers

  1. First time

    Connect your wallet and create a passkey.

  2. After that: Touch ID

    Check the site and the amount, then confirm.

  • Pays only the site the buyer is on.

    The payee is the wallet registered for the origin the buyer's browser signed, never an address from the page.

  • The relayer cannot redirect a payment.

    It only submits what the buyer's passkey signed. Its attestor key registers a site's payout after the DNS check: a new site is live at once, and a change to an existing payout waits 1 hour, during which the current payout wallet can freeze it.

  • No admin key.

    No owner, no upgrade and no pause. The contract never holds funds: USDT moves straight from the buyer to the site's payout wallet.

  • Limits.

    Testnet only, not audited. The frame shows the site the browser reports; Sitebound does not vouch for what a site sells.
