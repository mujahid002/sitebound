How it works

Register your site once, paste one tag, and every payment goes to the wallet your site registered.

In order: your browser creates a wallet. You add a TXT record named _sitebound.0xmujahid.xyz with the value sitebound= followed by your wallet, and the relayer checks it and registers 0xmujahid.xyz to that wallet on Whitechain. You paste the tag <sitebound-pay amount="12.00" order="A123">, which holds no address. When a buyer pays, a frame on another origin shows the site name the browser reports. Touch ID signs the payment with a passkey, the contract checks that signature on chain and pays only the wallet registered for that site. The buyer has no gas to pay, only a 0.01 USDT fee, and the relayer pays the gas.

What is Sitebound?

Checkout for USDT with no payout address on the page. The buyer confirms the site and the amount with Touch ID, like 3-D Secure for cards, and a contract pays the wallet registered for that site.

Do I need a crypto wallet, or to know crypto?

Site owners: no. Setup creates the payout wallet in your browser, so back it up, because it lives only there. Buyers link a wallet that holds USDT once, then pay with Touch ID.

Do my buyers need gas?

No. Buyers pay in USDT only, never WBT for gas. A relayer pays the gas and takes a 0.01 USDT fee.

Who gets the money?

Only the wallet, or the split of wallets, registered on-chain for that site's domain. The pay() call has no payee field, so a page cannot name another wallet.

Can a hacked page steal the payment?

It cannot redirect the money. It can still show a wrong amount, and a fake lookalike site can fool a buyer, so check the site and the amount in the confirmation. A site that signs quotes closes the amount gap.

What stops someone registering my domain?

A DNS TXT record that only the domain's owner can add. One trusted checker, our relayer, reads it today, and that is a real trust assumption. A change to an existing site's payout waits 1 hour, and its owner can freeze it.

Is it real money?

No. It runs on Whitechain Sepolia testnet with test USDT that has no value. It is not audited, so do not use it for real funds.

Why Whitechain?

Stablecoin payments settle in one transaction, and its USDT supports permit, which lets a relayer pay the gas. The chain's P-256 precompile also lets the contract verify Touch ID signatures itself.

Is it open source?

The contract's source is verified on Sourcify and the Whitechain explorer, so you can read exactly what runs. Verify us checks the live code in your browser.
