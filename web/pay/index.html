Take a payment on your own site

Enter your registered site, an amount and an order id. You get a snippet to paste and a pay page to host.

Sitebound pays the site your buyer's browser reports as the top-level page, and the passkey signs that origin, so a page on our domain can only ever pay our domain. That is the security property: a payment can only go to the site the buyer is actually on, so the pay page has to live on your domain.

One page then serves every invoice, but anyone can edit the link. Check the amount in your dashboard before you fulfil an order.
